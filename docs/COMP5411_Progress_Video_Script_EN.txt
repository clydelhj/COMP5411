4. Terrain Geometry and Materials
1:10 to 1:50 | 40 s | Slide 4

Here is the current viewer. I can orbit, pan, and move from the regional view toward the surface. Nearby patches receive finer geometry, while distant patches remain coarser. Adjacent levels share compatible boundaries, and the previous coverage stays visible while new patches load. The surface uses category-based PBR materials with triplanar mapping and blended transitions. Randomized texture sampling, mipmaps, and anisotropic filtering reduce repetition and moire at shallow viewing angles. The five-metre setting is a display limit, not the source resolution.
